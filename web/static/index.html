AI assistants that read your email can be tricked

Who authorised this action?

An email or web page can hide instructions that trick an AI assistant into sending your money or files to a stranger. WARRANT only lets the assistant use what you actually asked for, so hidden instructions can’t make it act.

      How WARRANT works

      1. Lock what you asked

      Before reading anything, it writes down what you asked for, who it may contact and what it may do, and locks that list.

      2. Read, but don’t obey

      The assistant reads emails and files freely. Instructions hidden inside them are just data. They don’t give orders.

      3. Check every value’s source

      Before any payment, email or share, each value must trace back to you or your own records. If it only came from hidden text, it’s blocked.
